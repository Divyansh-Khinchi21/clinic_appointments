💡 One-Page Product Landing Brief

Spec Breakdown: Eliminating front-desk double bookings & enforcing fair cancellation policies.

🎯

1. What It Is

CarePulse is an end-to-end clinic front-desk management portal. It connects patients and clinic staff to a central conflict-free booking system, preventing double-booked doctors, enabling instant patient lookups, and managing cancellation fees fairly.

⚡

2. Key Features

  • Conflict-Free Booking: Strictly blocks overlapping slots for the same doctor.
  • Fair Cancellation Policy: Free cancellation in good time (2+ hours in advance); small ₹150 fee for late cancels.
  • Doctor's Day Schedule Grid: Full daily slot visibility for front-desk receptionists.
  • Instant Patient Lookup: Search any appointment by patient name or phone.
  • Emergency Priority Override: Rapid slot reservation for urgent triage.
👥

3. Target Audience

  • Clinic Front-Desk Staff: Needing an automated, error-free schedule register.
  • Patients & Families: Seeking transparent booking and fair cancellation policies.
  • Doctors & Specialists: Wanting clear day timelines without slot overlaps.
📈

4. How It Helps

Eliminates double-booking errors by 100%, cuts front-desk phone friction, ensures doctors' day schedules run smoothly, and deters last-minute no-shows with transparent late cancellation fees.

🚀 3 Features We Would Build Next (Product Roadmap)

1. Automated WhatsApp & SMS Cancellation Alerts

Direct WhatsApp API notifications reminding patients 3 hours before their slot so they can cancel for FREE if needed, avoiding late fees.

2. AI Front Desk Voice & Chat Assistant

An AI voice bot capable of answering phone calls, checking doctor availability in real-time, and booking conflict-free slots automatically.

3. Instant Tele-Consultation Room Generator

Encrypted WebRTC video calls automatically spawned for remote patients unable to visit the physical clinic address.
