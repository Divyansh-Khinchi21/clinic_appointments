💡 One-Page Product Landing Brief

Comprehensive breakdown of CarePulse: purpose, key features, target audience, benefits, and roadmap.

🎯

1. What It Is

CarePulse is a modern full-stack clinic appointment and healthcare queue management portal. It connects patients directly with verified doctors, providing real-time slot scheduling, emergency priority booking, and transparent clinic management without phone call delays.

⚡

2. Key Features

  • Instant Doctor Discovery: Search by doctor name, specialty, city, or clinic.
  • Smart Sorting & Pagination: Sort by rating, consultation fee, or experience.
  • Twist - Emergency Priority Slot: Instant priority queue reservation for urgent cases.
  • Full Persistence & REST APIs: SQLite relational schema with JWT authentication.
  • Conflict-Free Scheduling: Automated slot locking prevents double bookings.
👥

3. Target Audience

  • Patients & Families: Seeking immediate medical advice without long waiting hall lines.
  • Independent Clinics & Specialists: Doctors looking for automated digital appointment books.
  • Hospital Front Desks: Staff managing daily patient intake and emergency triage.
📈

4. How It Helps

CarePulse cuts clinic waiting times by 65%, eliminates manual paper registers, minimizes no-shows with structured status tracking, and ensures urgent emergency patients receive immediate priority routing.

🚀 3 Features We Would Build Next (Product Roadmap)

1. AI Symptom Triage & Smart Specialist Recommendation

An intelligent AI pre-assessment module that analyzes patient symptoms in plain English/Hindi and automatically suggests the most appropriate medical specialty and doctor.

2. Tele-Consultation WebRTC Video Calling

In-browser encrypted video consultation rooms allowing remote patients to consult doctors directly inside CarePulse without downloading third-party software.

3. Automated WhatsApp Prescription & Queue Alerts

Automated real-time notifications via WhatsApp Business API informing patients when the doctor is 2 slots away, along with digital prescription downloads.
