💡 Product Overview & Architecture

Conflict-free clinic scheduling, fair late-cancellation handling, doctor day views, and patient search.

🎯

1. What It Is

CarePulse is a full-stack clinic management system built with Node.js, Express, SQLite, and REST APIs. It ensures doctors are never double-booked using strict backend time-interval overlap algorithms.

⚡

2. Key Features

  • Interval Overlap Validation: `(new_start < existing_end AND new_end > existing_start)` check blocks overlapping slots.
  • Fair Late-Cancellation Rule: >2 hours in advance = ₹0 fee; <=2 hours in advance = ₹100 fee.
  • Doctor's Day View: Front desk schedule view ordered by start time.
  • Patient Search: Fast lookup by patient name or phone.
  • Pagination & Sorting: `?page=1&limit=10` and `?sortBy=start_time&order=asc`.
👥

3. Target Audience

  • Front Desk / Staff: Seeking error-free schedule registers and quick patient lookups.
  • Patients: Wanting clear appointment start/end times and transparent cancellation rules.
  • Doctors: Requiring conflict-free daily schedule timelines.
📈

4. How It Helps

Eliminates double-booking errors by 100%, reduces phone call congestion, ensures fair cancellation terms for both patients and doctors, and provides real-time audit logging.
